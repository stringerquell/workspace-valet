A workstation is one desk position: laptop or desktop, monitors, keyboard, mouse, phone, and dock.

Questions worth asking before someone touches your equipment.

What counts as a workstation?

One desk position: a laptop or desktop, its monitors, keyboard, mouse, desk phone, and dock. Conference rooms and reception stations are counted by their equipment, and we confirm the full count with you before you reserve a date.

Why half price?

We're new in Baltimore, and the first four offices are helping us document the standard. You get the full service at half price. We get honest feedback and approved proof of the work.

Don't our cleaners already do this?

They may wipe some surfaces, and you should ask. The difference is ownership: our entire service is built around device-specific products, a manufacturer-aligned protocol, cloth control, privacy, and a documented result at every workstation.

Will it disrupt the workday?

Very little. Most workstations take approximately 10–12 minutes, and we coordinate the office by zone. Larger teams can schedule a quiet afternoon, before opening, or another low-traffic window.

What about our data?

We never request access. Devices are shut down whenever possible or locked under an agreed protocol. We never ask for a password, open a file, or photograph a screen.

Will this extend the life of our equipment?

We do not promise a longer device lifespan, and we do not replace IT maintenance or repair. We remove external dust, oils, and loose debris from the areas people touch and the openings we can safely reach, while flagging visible condition issues for your team.
